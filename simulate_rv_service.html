Knowledge Benchmarking

Flow: the lead picks Send to KB for Skill Upgrading for an employee → Resource Vision compares required and current levels in the shared skills database → it counts days from today's date (the date the lead clicks send) to the project start and splits them 85% training / 15% buffer → it sends KB the training days, buffer days, the number of employees fully matched, with one gap and with several gaps, and every lacking skill with its current and target level. When training completes the new levels are written back to the shared database. Afterwards the lead selects directly or books an interview. If the KB service is unavailable the request stays pending and can be retried.

Business rules

Unconfirmed projects: stages 1–5 booking only; stage 6 booking and blocking.
Booking: soft selection, no hours deducted, no hold, no block.
Interview: now or after confirmation, lead decides. Booking an interview deducts no hours; 7-day expiry.
Direct select: hours assigned as entered after the final capacity check.
Only confirmed allocations deduct hours, immediately.
Level order: Foundational < Applied < Advanced < Expert. At or above required = green; lower or missing = red.
KB window: days from today (send date) to project start, 85% training / 15% buffer.
Day 0 = today's real date. Project starts 01 Jan 2027 (day ).
